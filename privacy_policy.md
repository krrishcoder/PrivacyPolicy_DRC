# DUCS PhD Scholar Workflow Portal — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 8, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
The Department of University Computer Science (DUCS) operates the **PhD Scholar Workflow Portal** ("the Portal"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information. By accessing the Portal, you agree to the practices described here.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to operate the Portal:

* **Account credentials:** Username and hashed password used to authenticate your session.
* **Academic records:** Enrolment details, supervisor assignments, co-supervisor assignments, thesis title, course work results, fellowship status, and related agenda submissions you create or that are created on your behalf.
* **Session data:** Temporary session tokens stored server-side to keep you logged in; these expire on logout or after a period of inactivity.
* **Usage logs:** Timestamps of login events and form submissions, retained for audit and security purposes.

> **Note:** We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data unrelated to your academic workflow.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
Your information is used solely to:

1. **Authenticate and authorise** access to the Portal.
2. **Display, process, and route** agenda submissions (new admissions, supervisor changes, course work results, fellowship extensions, etc.) to the appropriate supervisors and administrators.
3. **Maintain an audit trail** required by university governance rules.
4. **Send official notices** related to your PhD programme.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

---

## 4. Data Sharing
Your data is shared only within DUCS on a **need-to-know basis**:

* **Supervisors and co-supervisors** can view agenda items assigned to them.
* **System administrators** have access to all records for support and governance purposes.
* **Third Parties:** No third parties outside the University of Delhi system receive your personal data unless required by law.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security
All data is stored on University-managed servers hosted within university infrastructure. We apply the following safeguards:

* **Hashing:** Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
* **Role-Based Access:** Access to the database is restricted by specific user permissions.
* **Session Integrity:** Sessions use server-side tokens; no sensitive data is stored in browser cookies.
* **Restricted Access:** The Portal is accessible only to authenticated users with valid DUCS credentials.

*Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, contact the DUCS administrator immediately.*

---

## 6. Data Retention
* **Academic Records:** Retained for the duration of your PhD programme and for a minimum of **five (5) years** after completion or withdrawal, in accordance with University of Delhi record-keeping requirements.
* **Audit Logs:** Login audit logs are retained for **two (2) years**.

---

## 7. Your Rights
You have the right to:

* **Access** the personal data we hold about you by contacting the DUCS administrator.
* **Correct** inaccurate records by submitting a request through your supervisor or directly to the administrator.
* **Request deletion** of non-essential data, subject to university record-retention obligations.

---

## 8. Cookies
The Portal uses a **single session cookie** to maintain your authenticated session. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are used.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or portal functionality. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the Portal after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
